instructions:
  - title: Cook the noodles
    text: Cook the udon according to the packet, drain and set aside.
  - title: Fry the aromatics
    text: Heat the chilli crisp in a large pan or wok over medium heat. Add the spring onion and garlic and fry for 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant, without letting the garlic burn.
  - title: Toss the noodles
    text: Add the noodles and toss until well coated in the garlic chilli oil.
  - title: Add the sauce
    text: Stir the soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil and sugar together, pour over the noodles and toss. Cook 2 to 3 minutes more so the noodles soak up the sauce, then taste and adjust.
  - title: Serve
    text: Serve hot, topped with more spring onion and sesame seeds.
