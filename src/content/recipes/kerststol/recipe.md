instructions:
  - title: Soak the fruit
    text: Soak the raisins in lukewarm water for 15 minutes. Drain, pat dry and mix with the chopped almonds.
  - title: Knead the dough
    text: Put all the dough ingredients except the butter in a bowl and knead for about 8 minutes until firm. Add the butter cube by cube and keep kneading until the dough is smooth and passes the windowpane test.
  - title: Add the fruit
    text: Rest the dough covered for 15 minutes, then knead the raisins and almonds through it.
  - title: First rise
    text: Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl and turn it to coat. Cover and leave at room temperature for 60 minutes; it will not quite double.
  - title: Make the almond paste roll
    text: Knead the lemon zest and beaten egg into the almond paste. Shape it into a log and set aside.
  - title: Knock back
    text: Turn the dough out onto an oiled surface and press out the air. Shape it loosely into a ball and rest it covered for about 20 minutes.
  - title: Shape
    text: Press the dough into an oval about 2 cm thick, leaving the short ends slightly thicker. Lay the almond paste log across the middle, fold the top over it past the bottom edge so it overlaps, and press down beside the log with the side of your hand. Press the sides gently closed.
  - title: Second rise
    text: Turn the loaf over onto a lined baking tray, cover and leave to rise for 45 minutes. It does not need to double.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake for 35 to 40 minutes in total, 20 minutes at 180 °C and then 15 to 20 minutes at 160 °C, until golden brown.
  - title: Finish
    text: Move the loaf to a rack. After 10 minutes brush it with melted butter and dust with icing sugar. Dust again with icing sugar just before serving.
