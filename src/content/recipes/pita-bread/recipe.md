ingredientGroups:
  - title: Ingredients
    ingredients:
      - name: Plain flour
        quantity: 500
        unit: g
      - name: Water
        quantity: 300
        unit: ml
        note: lukewarm
      - name: Olive oil
        quantity: 15
        unit: ml
      - name: Dried yeast
        quantity: 5
        unit: g
        note: about 1 tablespoon
      - name: Salt
        quantity: 6
        unit: g
instructions:
  - title: Activate the yeast
    text: Stir the yeast into the lukewarm water and leave for a few minutes.
  - title: Knead
    text: Knead the flour, olive oil and yeast water into a smooth dough. Add the salt only at the end and knead it through briefly, so it does not sit directly on the yeast.
  - title: Shape
    text: Divide the dough into 6 equal pieces and roll each into a tight ball by cupping your hand over it and rotating it on the worktop.
  - title: Flatten and prove
    text: On a floured surface, press each ball into a flat round dusted with a little flour on both sides. Lay them on lined baking trays with space between, cover with a tea towel and leave to rise for 30 to 40 minutes.
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: Meanwhile preheat the oven to 230 °C, top and bottom heat.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake for about 10 minutes until lightly golden; they puff up in the first 5 minutes. Press gently flat once out of the oven if they stay domed, and let them cool briefly.
source:

---

If the dough is very sticky, work in a little extra flour. Heat the oven as hot as it will go, and use a pizza stone if you have one, so the pitas puff properly.
