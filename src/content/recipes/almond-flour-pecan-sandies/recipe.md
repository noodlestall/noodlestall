instructions:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: Preheat the oven to 180 °C and line a large baking sheet with baking paper.
  - title: Blend butter and almond flour
    text: Beat the almond flour and butter on low speed with a mixer until no clumps remain and a soft dough forms.
  - title: Add the rest
    text: Add the chopped pecans, icing sugar, salt, baking powder and vanilla. Mix on low again until the dough comes together.
  - title: Shape
    text: Drop balls of dough onto the sheet, no more than 8 per tray. Press a pecan half into the top of each ball so it flattens slightly.
  - title: Bake and cool
    text: Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until the edges just start to colour. Rest on the tray for at least 10 minutes before moving to a rack to cool completely.

Chop the pecans very finely (a food processor helps), as larger chunks stop the dough from holding together. For a smoother finish, roll the dough into balls between your hands before baking.
