---
title: Socca
description: The chickpea pancake of Nice, nothing more than chickpea flour, water, olive oil and salt fried thin and crisp in a hot pan.
category: snacks
cuisine: french
difficulty: easy
servings: 2
prepTimeMinutes: 15
cookTimeMinutes: 10
coverImage: ./cover.webp
coverAlt: Thin golden chickpea pancake with crisp blistered edges, torn into pieces and dusted with black pepper
tags:
  - chickpea
  - pancake
  - vegan
  - gluten-free
  - quick
ingredientGroups:
  - title: Ingredients
    ingredients:
      - name: Chickpea flour
        quantity: 100
        unit: g
      - name: Salt
        quantity: 3
        unit: g
      - name: Water
        quantity: 250
        unit: ml
        note: cold
      - name: Extra virgin olive oil
        quantity: 45
        unit: ml
        note: plus more for the pan
      - name: Black pepper
        note: freshly ground
instructions:
  - title: Mix the batter
    text: Pour the cold water into a bowl and stir in the chickpea flour, then the olive oil and salt. Stir briskly until there are no lumps; the batter should be the consistency of cream.
  - title: Rest
    text: Leave the batter for about 10 minutes so the flour thickens.
  - title: Heat the pan
    text: Heat a little olive oil in a frying pan.
  - title: Fry
    text: Pour in the batter so it is 1 to 2 mm thick. Fry on one side only; bubbles are a good sign. Serve as soon as it is golden brown, with freshly ground pepper.
