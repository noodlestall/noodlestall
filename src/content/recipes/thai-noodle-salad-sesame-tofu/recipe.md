instructions:
  - title: Press and marinate the tofu
    text: Drain the tofu and press it dry with kitchen paper. Whisk the soy sauce, rice vinegar, maple syrup, sesame oil and ginger in a bowl, cut the tofu into 1 cm triangles or cubes and marinate for 20 minutes or overnight.
  - title: Prepare the coating
    text: Stir the ground flaxseed into 60 ml water and leave for 5 minutes to thicken. Put the flour and sesame seeds on two separate plates. Preheat the oven to 220 °C.
  - title: Coat the tofu
    text: Dip each piece of tofu in flour, then in the flax mixture, then roll in sesame seeds. Lay on a lined baking tray.
  - title: Bake
    text: Spray the tofu lightly with oil on both sides and bake for about 25 minutes, turning halfway.
  - title: Noodles and salad
    text: Meanwhile soak or cook the noodles according to the packet. Toss the salad leaves, carrots, spring onions, cucumber, radishes and coriander in a large bowl.
  - title: Blend the peanut sauce
    text: Blend or shake all the sauce ingredients until smooth, thinning with a little water if needed.
  - title: Assemble
    text: Divide the salad between bowls, add the noodles and sesame tofu, drizzle with peanut sauce and top with roasted peanuts and sliced chilli.
