instructions:
  - title: Make the tomato broth
    text: Mix the tomato purée and 2 pinches of salt with 1 litre of water and set aside. Slice the garlic.
  - title: Fry the garlic and chilli
    text: Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Fry the chilli flakes and garlic until the garlic starts to colour, then add the passata and let it reduce for 1 minute.
  - title: Fry the spaghetti
    text: Lay the spaghetti in a single layer on the passata. Fry for about 1 minute without stirring until golden, then turn carefully with a spatula and repeat. The passata should almost completely cook away into the pasta.
  - title: Cook with the broth
    text: Add 2 to 3 ladles of tomato broth so the pasta is just covered and let it reduce. Repeat until the spaghetti is cooked just past al dente.
  - title: Serve
    text: Twist the spaghetti into nests and serve scattered with chopped parsley.
