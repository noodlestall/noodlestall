instructions:
  - title: Fry the tofu and potatoes
    text: Heat the oil in a large frying pan or wok and put a small pan of water on to boil. Fry the tofu a few minutes each side until brown and crisp and move to a plate. Fry the potatoes in the same pan until hot and starting to crisp, add to the plate and let cool.
  - title: Boil the eggs and beans
    text: Boil the eggs for 7 minutes, then drop into cold water. Refill the pan with fresh water, bring to the boil and cook the beans 2 to 3 minutes until just tender. Drain and rinse under cold water.
  - title: Make the dressing
    text: Mash the peanut butter and kecap manis together with a fork until smooth, then whisk in the remaining dressing ingredients.
  - title: Assemble
    text: Pile the tofu, potatoes, beans, cabbage, cucumber, beansprouts, carrot and coriander in a bowl or on a platter and drizzle over half the dressing, keeping the rest for the table. Crumble over the prawn crackers, top with the peeled, quartered eggs and the crispy onions. Toss just before serving.
