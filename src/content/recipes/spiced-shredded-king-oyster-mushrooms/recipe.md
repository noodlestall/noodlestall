instructions:
  - title: Shred the mushrooms
    text: Press the tines of a fork into the top of a mushroom and drag downwards to pull away strands. Rotate the mushroom and repeat until it is shredded, then do the same with the others.
  - title: Season
    text: Put the shreds in a bowl and stir in the chilli powder, cumin, garlic granules and paprika until evenly coated.
  - title: Fry
    text: Warm a little oil in a frying pan over medium heat and soften the chopped garlic for a few seconds. Add the mushrooms and fry gently, stirring so nothing sticks. Once they release their liquid and are hot through, add a dash of mushroom soy, take the pan off the heat and give it one last stir.
  - title: Serve
    text: Pile the warm mixture into a sandwich, for example a soft bun with vegan mayo and salad, or toasted sourdough with tomato and leaves.

The shreds also work in tacos, wraps or on top of a burger. Swap the spice mix for barbecue seasoning for a different flavour. The fresh garlic and mushroom soy are our own additions to the original.
