instructions:
  - title: Activate the yeast
    text: Stir the yeast and sugar into the lukewarm water and leave for 5 minutes until it foams.
  - title: Make the dough
    text: Mix the yeast water into the flour until a rough dough forms. Add the soft butter and knead until the dough is smooth.
  - title: First proof
    text: Cover the dough and leave it for about 1.5 hours, until doubled in size.
  - title: Cook the aromatics
    text: Fry the diced onion in a few tablespoons of oil over medium-low heat until lightly golden. Add the garlic and scallions and keep stirring until everything is golden brown. Set aside, oil included, and let it cool.
  - title: Season the chicken
    text: Mix the ground chicken with the five spice, black pepper, soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce and dark soy sauce.
  - title: Finish the filling
    text: Add the chicken stock a little at a time, stirring until each addition is absorbed. Mix in the cooled aromatics.
  - title: Knock back and divide
    text: Punch the risen dough down and knead it on the work surface for 6 to 8 minutes until smooth. Roll it into an even log, cut into 16 pieces and keep them covered.
  - title: Roll the wrappers
    text: Flatten a piece of dough, then roll the edge back and forth with a rolling pin while turning the dough with your other hand. You want a round wrapper with a thick middle and a thin edge.
  - title: Fill and pleat
    text: Put some filling in the middle and pinch the edge to make the first pleat. Stretch, fold and pinch your way around the edge until the bun is closed.
  - title: Shape
    text: Set each bun on a square of parchment paper and shape it a little taller so it does not flatten.
  - title: Second rise
    text: Place the buns in the steamer and let them rise for another 20 minutes, until about 1.5 times the size.
  - title: Steam
    text: Steam over medium heat for 20 minutes, counting from when the water is at a full boil.
