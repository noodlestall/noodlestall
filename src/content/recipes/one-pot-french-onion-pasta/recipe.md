instructions:
  - title: Caramelise the onions
    text: Melt the butter in a heavy-bottomed pan over medium heat. Add the onions, olive oil and a good pinch of salt and cook for 20 minutes, stirring often, until soft and starting to brown.
  - title: Add the aromatics
    text: Stir in the garlic and thyme and cook for 3 more minutes until fragrant.
  - title: Deglaze
    text: Pour in the balsamic vinegar and white wine, scraping up everything stuck to the bottom of the pan.
  - title: Cook the pasta
    text: Add the rigatoni, Worcestershire sauce and broth and stir. Push the pasta under the liquid, cover and simmer over medium heat for 20 minutes, lifting the lid now and then to stir so nothing sticks.
  - title: Finish
    text: Once the pasta is al dente, stir in the Gruyère and cream until creamy. Taste and season with flaky salt and pepper.
