instructions:
  - title: Roast the chickpeas
    text: Heat the oven to 200 °C. Toss the chickpeas with the oil, paprika and salt on a tray. Roast for 20 to 25 minutes, shaking once, until crisp.
  - title: Massage the kale
    text: Put the kale in a large bowl with the olive oil and a pinch of salt. Rub the leaves with your hands for a minute until they soften and darken.
  - title: Make the dressing
    text: Whisk the tahini, lemon juice, water, garlic, maple syrup and salt until smooth. Add a little more water if it is too thick to pour.
  - title: Assemble
    text: Toss the kale with the cucumber and most of the dressing. Top with the warm chickpeas and pumpkin seeds and drizzle over the rest.
