instructions:
  - title: Prepare the fish and aromatics
    text: Cut the cod into pieces of about 7 cm. Mince the shallots and garlic and finely chop the chillies. Shred the spring onions and soak them in cold water for 5 minutes so they curl.
  - title: Marinate
    text: Mix the fish sauce, black pepper and oil in a bowl, add the cod and turn gently to coat.
  - title: Make the caramel
    text: Melt the sugar in a clay pot over medium-low heat, stirring now and then, until it turns dark amber after about 5 minutes.
  - title: Fry the aromatics
    text: Stir a splash of oil into the caramel, then add the shallots and garlic and fry for a couple of minutes until fragrant.
  - title: Build the sauce
    text: Add the fish sauce and coconut soda, bring to a simmer and let it reduce slightly.
  - title: Braise the fish
    text: Add the cod with its marinade, then the chillies and black pepper. Return to a simmer, cover and cook for 10 minutes.
  - title: Serve
    text: Top with the spring onion curls and more black pepper. Serve with steamed white rice.
