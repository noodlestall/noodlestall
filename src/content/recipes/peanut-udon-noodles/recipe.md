  - title: Make the sauce
    text: Stir the peanut butter, soy sauces, sugar, sesame oil, chilli crisp, ginger and garlic together in a bowl. Loosen with the reserved water a tablespoon at a time until pourable.
  - title: Toss
    text: Add the noodles to the sauce and mix until evenly coated.
