instructions:
  - title: Prepare
    text: Heat the oven to 160 °C. Line an 18 cm springform tin with baking paper.
  - title: Mix the dry ingredients
    text: Stir the almond flour and baking powder together in a bowl.
  - title: Make the batter
    text: Add the olive oil, honey, vanilla, eggs and lemon zest and mix until smooth.
  - title: Add the apples
    text: Core the apples and dice them with the skin on. Fold the pieces through the batter.
  - title: Fill the tin
    text: Spread the batter in the tin and press it down lightly. Scatter the flaked almonds on top.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake in the middle of the oven for 65 to 75 minutes.
  - title: Cool
    text: Leave the cake to cool in the tin for about 20 minutes before releasing it.
