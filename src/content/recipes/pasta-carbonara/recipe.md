instructions:
  - title: Crisp the pancetta
    text: Put the pancetta and about 120 ml of the water in a large non-stick pan and bring to a simmer over medium-high heat. Once the water has evaporated, after 6 to 7 minutes, turn the heat to medium-low and cook 6 to 8 minutes more until brown and crisp.
  - title: Fry the garlic
    text: Tip the pancetta into a sieve set over a bowl, leaving about a teaspoon of fat in the pan. Return the pan to the heat and fry the garlic for 30 seconds until fragrant and lightly golden.
  - title: Make the egg mixture
    text: Scrape the garlic into a mixing bowl with a tablespoon of the drained pancetta fat. Add the eggs, egg yolk, parmesan and pepper and whisk until smooth.
  - title: Cook the pasta
    text: Bring the remaining water to the boil in a large pot; use no more than this so the water stays starchy. Add the spaghetti and salt and cook until al dente. Set a colander inside a large bowl.
  - title: Drain
    text: Drain the pasta into the colander so the water collects in the bowl. Keep 240 ml of the hot water, discard the rest and put the pasta into the now empty bowl.
  - title: Bring it together
    text: Slowly whisk 120 ml of the pasta water into the egg mixture, then pour it over the pasta while tossing. Add the pancetta and toss again, seasoning with salt if needed.
  - title: Rest and serve
    text: Let the pasta sit 2 to 4 minutes, tossing often, until the sauce thickens and coats the strands. Loosen with the remaining pasta water as needed and serve at once with more parmesan and the parsley.

Use freshly grated Parmigiano Reggiano; it melts better and tastes richer. The pasta water must be very hot when it goes into the eggs so the sauce cooks through.
