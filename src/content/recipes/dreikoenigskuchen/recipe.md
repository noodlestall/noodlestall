title: Dreikönigskuchen
description: The Swiss Epiphany crown of soft sultana-studded yeast buns around a central one, with a hidden king. Whoever finds it wears the paper crown.
category: baking
cuisine: swiss
difficulty: medium
servings: 8
prepTimeMinutes: 150
cookTimeMinutes: 30
draft: true
tags:
  - yeast dough
  - sultanas
  - epiphany

  - vegetarian
ingredientGroups:
  - title: Dough
    ingredients:
      - name: Sultanas
        quantity: 100
        unit: g
      - name: Plain flour
        quantity: 500
        unit: g
      - name: Sugar
        quantity: 36
        unit: g
      - name: Salt
        quantity: 6
        unit: g
        note: 1 to 1½ teaspoons
      - name: Butter
        quantity: 75
        unit: g
        note: soft
      - name: Fresh yeast
        quantity: 30
        unit: g
        note: crumbled
      - name: Milk
        quantity: 250
        unit: ml
      - name: King figurine or whole almond
        quantity: 1
        unit: piece
  - title: Topping
    ingredients:
      - name: Egg yolk
        quantity: 1
        unit: piece
      - name: Milk
        quantity: 15
        unit: ml
        note: to mix with the yolk
      - name: Flaked almonds
        note: 2 tablespoons
      - name: Pearl sugar
        note: a little
instructions:
  - title: Soak the sultanas
    text: Cover the sultanas with hot water and leave to soak.
  - title: Make the dough
    text: Mix the flour, sugar and salt and make a well. Add the butter, yeast and milk and knead to a smooth, supple dough.
  - title: First rise
    text: Cover and leave at room temperature until doubled in size.
  - title: Add the sultanas
    text: Drain the sultanas and knead them into the dough.
  - title: Second rise
    text: Cover again and leave until doubled once more.
  - title: Shape the crown
    text: Roll a quarter of the dough into a ball and set it in the middle of a lined baking tray. Cut the rest into 8 pieces, roll into balls and hide the king or almond in one of them. Arrange them around the centre with a little space between.
  - title: Prove
    text: Cover with a damp cloth and leave for 15 minutes.
  - title: Glaze
    text: Brush with the egg yolk mixed with milk, then scatter over the flaked almonds and pearl sugar.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake in the lower part of the oven at 180°C for 25 to 30 minutes.
