instructions:
  - title: Caramelise the sugar
    text: Melt the palm sugar in a small saucepan over medium heat, swirling and stirring for 1 to 2 minutes until caramelised.
  - title: Finish the sauce
    text: Carefully add the tamarind paste, fish sauce and water. Simmer until the sugar dissolves again, then take off the heat and let cool.
  - title: Soak the noodles
    text: Soak the noodles in room-temperature water for about an hour until just pliable. Drain.
  - title: Season the wok
    text: Heat 60 ml of the oil in a wok over high heat until smoking, then pour it out.
  - title: Prepare the tofu mix
    text: Combine the tofu, shallots, garlic, chilli flakes, dried shrimp and preserved daikon in a bowl.
  - title: Cook the prawns
    text: Add 30 ml oil to the hot wok and cook the prawns for 2 minutes until pink. Remove and set aside.
  - title: Fry the tofu mix
    text: Add the remaining oil and the tofu mixture. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the shallots are golden and translucent.
  - title: Add the noodles
    text: Add the noodles and half the sauce. Toss for 1 to 2 minutes until the sauce is mostly absorbed.
  - title: Scramble the eggs
    text: Push the noodles to one side, crack in the eggs and scramble lightly, then fold them through the noodles.
  - title: Finish
    text: Return the prawns and toss to heat through. Off the heat, stir in the chives, bean sprouts and half the peanuts.
  - title: Serve
    text: Serve with lime wedges, the remaining peanuts and extra chilli flakes, sprouts and chives on the side.
