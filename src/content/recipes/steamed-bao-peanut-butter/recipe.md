instructions:
  - title: Make the starter
    text: Combine the yeast, sugar, flour and warm water in a large mixing bowl and leave to stand for 30 minutes.
  - title: Knead and prove
    text: Add the remaining dough ingredients and knead until elastic, adding a touch more flour if it is too sticky. Cover and leave somewhere warm until tripled in size, about 1½ hours.
  - title: Shape the buns
    text: Knead the dough on a well-floured surface for 5 minutes. Divide it into twelve equal pieces and roll into balls.
  - title: Fill
    text: Flatten each ball to about palm size, put a tablespoon of peanut butter in the centre and pinch the edges firmly shut. Place seam side down on a small square of baking paper.
  - title: Steam
    text: Steam the buns until puffed and cooked through, about 2 minutes in a microwave steamer or longer in a steamer over boiling water. A splash of vinegar in the steaming water keeps them white. Eat hot.
