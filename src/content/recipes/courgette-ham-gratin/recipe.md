ingredientGroups:
  - title: Ingredients
    ingredients:
      - name: Courgettes
        quantity: 8
        unit: piece
        note: small
      - name: Ham
        quantity: 200
        unit: g
        note: finely chopped
      - name: Salt
        note: a good pinch
      - name: Lemon juice
        note: a little
      - name: Cream
        quantity: 200
        unit: ml
      - name: Grated cheese
        quantity: 50
        unit: g
      - name: Butter
        note: a little, for the dish
instructions:
  - title: Prepare the courgettes
    text: Peel the courgettes and cut them lengthwise into quarters. Rub them with lemon juice.
  - title: Line the dish
    text: Butter a gratin dish and cover the base with the chopped ham.
  - title: Assemble
    text: Lay the courgette quarters on top, cut side down. Salt lightly, pour over the cream and scatter with the grated cheese.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake for 15 minutes covered, then 15 minutes uncovered.
  - title: Serve
    text: Serve with rice or potatoes and a seasonal salad.
---

If the courgettes are large and a little firm, blanch them for 5 to 10 minutes in salted water before assembling.
