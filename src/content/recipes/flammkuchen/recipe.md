instructions:
  - title: Make the dough
    text: Mix the flour and salt in a bowl. Pour in the water and oil and knead to a soft, smooth dough. Shape into a ball and rest, covered, at room temperature for about 30 minutes.
  - title: Roll out
    text: Heat the oven to 240 °C. Halve the dough and roll each piece into an oval about 3 mm thick on a lightly floured surface. Lay them on two baking trays lined with baking paper.
  - title: Top
    text: Spread the crème fraîche over the dough, leaving a border of about 1 cm. Peel the onions and slice into fine rings, cut the bacon into strips and scatter both over the crème fraîche. Season with pepper.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake one tray at a time on the lowest shelf for about 12 minutes each.
