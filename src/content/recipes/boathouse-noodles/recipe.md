instructions:
  - title: Make the dough
    text: Mix the flour, water and salt and knead into a smooth ball. Rest it for 5 to 10 minutes, cut it into six even pieces, roll each into a short log and coat in vegetable oil. Rest for another 30 minutes.
  - title: Pull and boil the noodles
    text: Bring a large pot of water to the boil. Slap and stretch each log out to arm's width, tear it in two lengthways with your fingers and boil for about 4 minutes.
  - title: Heat the oil
    text: Meanwhile heat a few spoonfuls of vegetable oil in a small pan until it is smoking hot.
  - title: Bowl the noodles
    text: Lift the noodles into a serving bowl. Do not drain them completely; they should stay coated in a little cooking water.
  - title: Dress
    text: Stir the sauce ingredients together and pour over the noodles.
  - title: Add the toppings
    text: Scatter the chopped spring onion, chilli and garlic over the top.
  - title: Sizzle
    text: Pour the hot oil over the toppings and noodles so they sizzle, then toss and eat at once.
