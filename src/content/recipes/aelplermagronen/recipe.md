      - name: Älplermagronen pasta
        quantity: 500

      - name: Gruyère

instructions:
  - title: Cook the potatoes
    text: Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil. Peel the potatoes, cut them into small batons and cook for 10 minutes.
  - title: Add the pasta
    text: Add the pasta to the potato water and cook for another 10 minutes, then drain everything and tip into a large gratin dish.
  - title: Add cheese and cream
    text: Scatter the grated Gruyère and crumbled stock cubes over the top, then pour over the warm cream.
  - title: Gratinate
    text: Bake in an oven preheated to 220 °C for 10 to 15 minutes until golden and bubbling.
  - title: Fry the onions
    text: Toss the onion rings in flour and fry in butter until golden and crisp.
  - title: Serve
    text: Pile the onions on the Älplermagronen and serve with apple sauce on the side.
