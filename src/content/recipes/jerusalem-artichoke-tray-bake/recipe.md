instructions:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: Heat the oven to 200°C fan.
  - title: Prepare
    text: Peel the artichokes and put them in a baking dish. Wash the lemon in hot water, dry it and finely grate the zest, then squeeze some juice and mix it with the stock. Chop the almonds and peel the garlic.
  - title: Assemble
    text: Scatter the garlic, lemon zest and almonds over the artichokes. Pour in the stock, drizzle with the oil and season with salt and pepper.
  - title: Roast
    text: Roast for about 60 minutes until the liquid has evaporated and the artichokes are nicely browned. Turn them as needed and season to taste before serving.
