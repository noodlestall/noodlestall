instructions:
  - title: Cook the pasta
    text: Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil and cook the penne until done.
  - title: Fry the vegetables
    text: Meanwhile finely chop the onion and garlic and dice half the courgette. Fry gently over low heat until soft.
  - title: Make the sauce
    text: Mix the yoghurt with the egg and lemon juice and season with salt and pepper. Chop the parsley and stir it in.
  - title: Flake the mackerel
    text: Pull the mackerel into flakes, removing every bone.
  - title: Combine
    text: Drain the pasta and immediately mix in the vegetables, mackerel and yoghurt sauce. Stir well and serve.
