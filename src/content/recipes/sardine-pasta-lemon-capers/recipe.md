instructions:
  - title: Start the sauce
    text: Put a large pot of water on to boil. Meanwhile heat the olive oil in a large sauté pan over medium heat and cook the shallots, lemon zest, pepper flakes and salt until the shallots are soft and translucent, without letting them burn.
  - title: Add the sardines
    text: Add the sardines and capers and sauté for about 2 minutes, breaking the sardines into smaller pieces. When they start to turn golden, slowly stir in the lemon juice and keep warm on the lowest heat.
  - title: Cook the pasta
    text: Cook the pasta in the boiling water according to the packet, then drain, reserving a cup of the cooking water.
  - title: Toss and serve
    text: Add the pasta and herbs to the sauce and toss to coat, loosening with a spoonful of pasta water at a time if it seems dry. Adjust the salt, lemon and chilli and serve hot.
