instructions:
  - title: Make the broth
    text: Put the chicken, water, onion, garlic, bay leaves and salt in a large pot. Bring to a boil, skim any foam, then simmer gently for 40 minutes.
  - title: Shred the chicken
    text: Lift out the chicken and set aside to cool slightly. Discard the onion, garlic and bay leaves. Pull the meat from the bones and shred it.
  - title: Cook the vegetables
    text: Return the broth to a simmer. Add the carrots and celery and cook for 10 minutes until tender.
  - title: Add the noodles
    text: Add the egg noodles and cook according to the packet, usually 6 to 8 minutes. Return the shredded chicken to the pot to warm through.
  - title: Season and serve
    text: Stir in the parsley and lemon juice. Season generously with black pepper and serve hot.
