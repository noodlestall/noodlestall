ingredientGroups:
  - title: Ingredients
    ingredients:
      - name: Garlic cloves
        quantity: 100
        unit: piece
        note: peeled, about 300 g or 8 whole bulbs
      - name: Neutral oil
        note: for frying
      - name: Mustard seeds
        note: to taste
      - name: Curry leaves
        note: a few sprigs
      - name: Warm water
        quantity: 100
        unit: ml
        note: plus a splash more if needed
      - name: Salt
        note: to taste
      - name: Curry spices
        note: ground, to taste
      - name: Tamarind paste
        note: to taste
      - name: Coconut milk
        note: 1 can
instructions:
  - title: Temper the spices
    text: Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat and add the mustard seeds. When they start to pop, add the curry leaves and the garlic cloves.
  - title: Braise the garlic
    text: Fry the garlic for a few minutes over slightly higher heat, then add the warm water, turn the heat to low and cover. Simmer for 15 to 20 minutes until the cloves are soft and slightly translucent, adding a splash of water if it looks dry.
  - title: Season
    text: Stir in the salt, spices and tamarind paste carefully so the soft cloves do not break up, and cover for a few more minutes. Add the coconut milk, stir gently and simmer for another 5 minutes.
  - title: Serve
    text: Serve with basmati rice or plain naan.
