title: Five-Spice Braised Tofu
description: Fried tofu slabs marinated and then simmered in a tamari and five-spice broth until firm and deeply savoury. Good hot or cold.
category: dinner
cuisine: chinese
difficulty: easy
servings: 4
prepTimeMinutes: 75
cookTimeMinutes: 20

tags:
  - tofu

  - five-spice
  - vegan

ingredientGroups:
  - title: Ingredients
    ingredients:
      - name: Extra-firm tofu
        quantity: 400
        unit: g
        note: pressed
      - name: Neutral oil
        note: a little, for frying
      - name: Vegetable stock
        quantity: 120
        unit: ml
      - name: Water
        quantity: 120
        unit: ml
      - name: Tamari
        quantity: 60
        unit: ml
        note: low-sodium
      - name: Coconut sugar
        quantity: 12
        unit: g
        note: or brown sugar
      - name: Chinese five-spice powder
        note: 1 teaspoon
instructions:
  - title: Press and cut
    text: Press the tofu for about 30 minutes if it is not already firm, then cut it into slabs.
  - title: Fry
    text: Heat a little oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat and fry the slabs for 3 to 5 minutes per side until golden. Set aside.
  - title: Marinate
    text: Mix the stock, water, tamari, sugar and five-spice in a pan. Add the tofu, cover and leave for 30 minutes, turning halfway.
  - title: Simmer
    text: Bring to a gentle simmer, cover and cook for 20 minutes, turning the tofu every 5 minutes.
  - title: Rest and slice
    text: Let the tofu cool for 10 minutes so it firms up, then slice and serve.
