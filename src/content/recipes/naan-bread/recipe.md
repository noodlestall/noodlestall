instructions:
  - title: Activate the yeast
    text: Sprinkle the yeast and half the sugar over the warm water and leave for 10 to 15 minutes until frothy.
  - title: Make the dough
    text: Mix the flour, remaining sugar, salt and baking powder in a large bowl. Make a well and pour in the melted butter, yoghurt, nigella seeds and yeast mixture, then bring it together with your hands. Add a spoonful of flour if very wet or a splash of warm water if dry; the dough should be very soft but form a ball.
  - title: Knead and prove
    text: Knead in the bowl, then on a well-floured surface for 10 minutes until smooth, elastic and still soft. Shape into a ball, put in a buttered bowl, cover and leave somewhere warm for about an hour until doubled.
  - title: Shape
    text: Divide the dough into six balls on a floured tray and cover with a damp tea towel. Roll one ball into a teardrop about 21 cm long and 13 cm at its widest.
  - title: Cook
    text: Heat a large non-stick frying pan over high heat. Lay the naan in the very hot dry pan and let it puff for about 3 minutes, then turn and cook 3 to 4 minutes more until cooked through and charred in patches.
  - title: Keep warm and serve
    text: Brush each cooked naan with melted butter or ghee and stack them on a baking sheet under foil in the oven at its lowest setting while you cook the rest. Serve warm.
