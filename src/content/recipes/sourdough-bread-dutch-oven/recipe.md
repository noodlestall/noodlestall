      - name: Flour
        quantity: 500
        unit: g
        note: for example 100 g wholemeal, 200 g spelt and 200 g white
      - name: Salt
        quantity: 10
        unit: g
      - name: Water
        quantity: 330
        unit: ml
instructions:
  - title: Make the preferment
    text: The evening before, or 8 to 24 hours ahead, mix the starter, flour and water in a small bowl with the back of a wooden spoon. Cover and leave at room temperature overnight; it can sit longer in the fridge.
  - title: Mix the dough
    text: The next morning, mix the preferment with the flour, salt and water in a large bowl. The dough should be fairly wet; wet your hands to fold it rather than adding much flour, and add a little more flour only if it feels too loose.
  - title: Rise
    text: Cover and leave at room temperature for about 8 hours. Fold the dough a couple of times during the rise if you like, but it is not essential.
  - title: Heat the pot
    text: Heat the oven with a lidded cast-iron pot inside to 230 °C. Turn the dough onto baking paper and dust the top with flour so the crust crackles, then lower the paper and dough into the hot pot and put the lid on.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake for 1 hour at 230 °C with the lid on.
---

Timings depend on the room temperature; everything takes longer in winter. Handle the dough with wet hands rather than floured ones.
