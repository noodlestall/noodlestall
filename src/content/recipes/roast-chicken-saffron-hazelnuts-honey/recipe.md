instructions:
  - title: Marinate
    text: Mix the chicken with the onions, olive oil, ginger, cinnamon, saffron, lemon juice, water, salt and pepper in a large bowl. Marinate for at least an hour, or overnight in the fridge.
  - title: Toast the hazelnuts
    text: Heat the oven to 190 °C. Spread the hazelnuts on a baking sheet and toast for 10 minutes until lightly browned. Chop coarsely.
  - title: Roast
    text: Tip the chicken and marinade onto a large baking sheet, skin side up, and roast for about 35 minutes.
  - title: Add the nut paste
    text: Mix the honey, rose water and hazelnuts into a rough paste. Spoon it generously over each piece of chicken and return to the oven for 5 to 10 minutes until cooked through and the nuts are golden.
  - title: Serve
    text: Transfer to a serving dish and scatter over the spring onions.
