instructions:
  - title: Prepare
    text: Heat the oven to 170 °C. Oil a 23 cm springform tin and line the base with baking paper.
  - title: Melt the chocolate
    text: Melt the chocolate in a bowl set over barely simmering water. Remove from the heat and stir in the olive oil.
  - title: Whisk the eggs
    text: In a large bowl whisk the sugar, eggs and vanilla for 3 minutes until pale and slightly thickened.
  - title: Combine
    text: Fold the chocolate mixture into the eggs. Stir the almonds, cocoa, baking powder and salt together, fold them in, then stir in the boiling water. The batter will be thin.
  - title: Bake
    text: Pour into the tin and bake for 40 to 45 minutes until the top is set and a skewer comes out with moist crumbs. Cool fully in the tin before releasing.
