instructions:
  - title: Fry the shallots
    text: Salt the sliced shallots on kitchen paper for 10 minutes, then pat dry. Fry them in hot oil until golden and crisp and drain on paper. Keep the oil for the chicken.
  - title: Pound the paste
    text: Toast the cumin, coriander seeds and peppercorns for about 20 seconds until fragrant. Pound them with the garlic and coriander roots in a mortar.
  - title: Make the marinade
    text: Put the paste in a large bowl with the palm sugar, fish sauce and salt. Add the tempura flour and work in the ice-cold water a little at a time until you have a smooth batter.
  - title: Marinate the chicken
    text: Slash the chicken twice around the bone and coat every piece in the marinade. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, ideally overnight.
  - title: Fry the chicken
    text: Heat the shallot oil to 175 to 190 °C and deep-fry the chicken until golden and cooked through, turning as needed. Drain on a rack, rest briefly and serve with the crispy shallots.
