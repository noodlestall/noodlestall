  - title: Tofu
    ingredients:
      - name: Firm tofu
        quantity: 450
        unit: g
        note: or ready-fried tofu
      - name: Neutral oil
        note: for deep-frying
  - title: Sauce
    ingredients:
      - name: Roma tomatoes
        quantity: 5
        unit: piece
        note: ripe, medium, cut into quarters or sixths
      - name: Tomato paste
        quantity: 15
        unit: g
      - name: Fish sauce
        quantity: 2.5
        unit: ml

      - name: Salt
        quantity: 6

      - name: Sugar
        quantity: 8
        unit: g

        quantity: 120

  - title: Garnish
    ingredients:
      - name: Spring onion
        quantity: 1
        unit: piece
        note: finely sliced
instructions:
  - title: Cut and press the tofu
    text: Halve the tofu blocks lengthwise, then cut into smaller pieces. Sandwich them between kitchen towels on a plate, weigh down with another plate and press for 20 to 30 minutes.
  - title: Fry the tofu
    text: Heat a pot of oil to 150 °C and fry the tofu in batches of 4 to 6 pieces until golden, moving them so they do not stick. Drain on kitchen paper.
  - title: Cut the tomatoes
    text: Cut the tomatoes into bite-sized pieces, 4 to 8 per tomato.
  - title: Simmer the sauce
    text: Put all the sauce ingredients in a medium pot, bring to the boil, then simmer gently for about 10 minutes until the tomatoes have collapsed and the sauce is as thick as you like.
  - title: Taste
    text: Adjust with more tomato paste, fish sauce, salt or sugar, especially if the tomatoes were not very ripe.
  - title: Finish
    text: Add the tofu and stir for a few minutes so it warms through and soaks up some sauce. Top with the spring onion and serve.
