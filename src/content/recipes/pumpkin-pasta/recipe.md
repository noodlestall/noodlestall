instructions:
  - title: Roast the pumpkin
    text: Heat the oven to 180 °C. Wash, deseed and dice the pumpkin, skin on. Toss with about a third of the olive oil, salt and pepper and roast on a lined tray for 15 to 20 minutes.
  - title: Cook the pasta
    text: Boil the spaghetti in salted water until al dente. When in doubt, take it out a minute early.
  - title: Prepare the rest
    text: Deseed and finely chop the chilli, chop the garlic, dice the drained tomatoes, grate the parmesan, roughly chop the walnuts and chop the parsley.
  - title: Make the base
    text: Heat the remaining olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Soften the chilli, garlic and tomatoes for 2 to 3 minutes, then stir in the tomato purée briefly.
  - title: Combine
    text: Lift the spaghetti straight from the water into the pan, dripping wet. Add the pumpkin, walnuts, parmesan and parsley, toss well and season with salt and pepper.

Hokkaido does not need peeling. Mildly marinated sun-dried tomatoes work best, but antipasti-style ones are fine too.
