instructions:
  - title: Fry the aromatics
    text: Warm the lard or oil in a large saucepan over low heat. Add the lemongrass, onion, shallots, galangal, garlic, lime leaves, peppercorns, coriander seeds and dried chillies and fry uncovered for about 15 minutes, stirring now and then, until the onion is translucent and everything is fragrant and lightly coloured.
  - title: Seal the beef
    text: Add the beef and stir thoroughly so the herbs and spices are mixed through. Cover, raise the heat slightly and cook for 10 minutes, stirring halfway, until all the beef looks sealed.
  - title: Add the pineapple
    text: Stir in the pineapple, cover again and turn up to medium heat. Cook for 15 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes or so, until the meat has released its juices.
  - title: Braise
    text: Add the mushroom soy sauce, oyster sauce, seasoning sauce, fish sauce, sugar, salt and 1.5 litres of the water. Stir, cover and cook on a low heat for 2½ hours, checking every half hour that nothing sticks.
  - title: Reduce
    text: Test the beef for tenderness, then add the remaining 500 ml of water and simmer uncovered for 30 minutes until the liquid sits below the meat and the sauce is rich. Stir gently, if at all, so the cheeks do not break up.
  - title: Finish
    text: Just before serving, scatter the torn lime leaves and spring onion lengths over the beef. Cover for a couple of minutes until wilted, then serve.
---

Beef cheeks need a long, slow cook for the fat to melt and the fibres to soften, and the pineapple helps that along; shin or oxtail work just as well. Use galangal, not ginger.
