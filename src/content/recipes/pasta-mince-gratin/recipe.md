instructions:
  - title: Cook the pasta
    text: Cook the pasta in salted water until al dente and drain.
  - title: Brown the mince
    text: Finely chop the onion and soften it in a little fat. Add the minced meat, brown it well and season.
  - title: Assemble
    text: Mix the pasta and meat together, tip into a baking dish and cover with the grated Gruyère.
  - title: Add the cream
    text: Season the cream with salt and pepper and pour it over the dish.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake at 200 °C for 15 to 20 minutes until golden on top.
