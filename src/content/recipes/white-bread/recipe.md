instructions:
  - title: Activate the yeast
    text: Mix the yeast, water and a pinch of the sugar in a large bowl. Rest for 5 to 10 minutes until foamy.
  - title: Make the dough
    text: Add the remaining sugar, the salt, oil and flour and mix. Knead until smooth and elastic and the dough pulls away from the bowl; it should barely stick to a clean finger. Add a little more flour if needed.
  - title: First rise
    text: Place the dough in an oiled bowl, turning to coat. Cover and leave in a warm place until doubled, about 1½ hours.
  - title: Shape and second rise
    text: Punch the dough down and place it in the tin or tube. Leave to rise again for 45 minutes to an hour.
  - title: Bake
    text: Heat the oven to 180 °C. Bake for 30 to 33 minutes until golden on top and hollow-sounding when tapped.
  - title: Cool
    text: Turn the loaf out onto a wire rack to cool.

To speed up the first rise, warm the oven to 40 °C, switch it off and let the covered dough rise inside with the door slightly open. The dough can also be made a day ahead and kept in the fridge before the first rise, then brought to room temperature.
