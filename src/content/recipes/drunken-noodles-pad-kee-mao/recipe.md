instructions:
  - title: Marinate the chicken
    text: Work the water into the sliced chicken with your hands until it is absorbed. Mix in the cornstarch, oil and soy sauce until evenly coated and set aside for 20 minutes.
  - title: Soak the noodles
    text: Prepare the rice noodles according to the packet, either soaking them in hot tap water for 20 minutes or boiling briefly until just al dente, then rinsing under cold water.
  - title: Mix the sauce
    text: Stir the dissolved brown sugar, both soy sauces, fish sauce, oyster sauce and white pepper together in a small bowl.
  - title: Sear the chicken
    text: Heat a wok until almost smoking and swirl in two thirds of the oil. Sear the chicken for 1 minute per side until about 90 percent cooked, then remove and set aside. Wash the wok if anything has stuck.
  - title: Fry the aromatics
    text: Return the wok to high heat with the remaining oil, garlic and ginger. After a few seconds add the shallots and stir-fry for 20 seconds.
  - title: Add the vegetables and noodles
    text: Add the spring onion, chillies, basil, baby corn and Shaoxing wine and stir-fry for 20 seconds. Add the noodles and toss with a scooping motion for about a minute until warmed through.
  - title: Sauce and finish
    text: Pour in the sauce and stir-fry on the highest heat for about 1 minute until the noodles are evenly coloured, scraping the bottom of the wok. Return the chicken, stir-fry for 1 to 2 minutes more and serve.
