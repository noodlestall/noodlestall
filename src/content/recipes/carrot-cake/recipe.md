instructions:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  - title: Prepare the tins
    text: Grease one or two cake tins and line the bases with baking paper. With a single tin you will split the cake in half later to frost between the layers.
  - title: Mix the dry ingredients
    text: Whisk the flour, baking soda, salt and cinnamon in a bowl until well blended.
  - title: Mix the wet ingredients
    text: In a separate bowl whisk the oil, both sugars and the vanilla. Add the eggs one at a time, whisking after each.
  - title: Combine
    text: Stir the dry ingredients gently into the wet until the batter is smooth, then stir in the carrots and pecans. Divide between the tins.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake for 35 to 45 minutes, until the tops spring back when touched and a skewer in the centre comes out clean. Cool completely.
  - title: Make the frosting
    text: Beat the cream cheese with a hand mixer for about a minute until smooth, then beat in the icing sugar.
  - title: Assemble
    text: Frost the top of one layer, set the second on it and spread the rest of the frosting over the top in swirls. Finish with a handful of pecans.
