instructions:
  - title: Cook the pasta
    text: Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil, add the salt and cook the spaghetti until just al dente. Reserve a cup of the cooking water before draining.
    image: ./step-01.svg
    imageAlt: Pot of boiling water with spaghetti (placeholder illustration)
  - title: Soften the garlic
    text: While the pasta cooks, warm the olive oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook gently for a minute until fragrant but not browned.
  - title: Burst the tomatoes
    text: Add the cherry tomatoes and a pinch of salt. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes, pressing them with a spoon as they soften, until they collapse into a loose sauce.
    image: ./step-02.svg
    imageAlt: Pan of softened cherry tomatoes (placeholder illustration)
  - title: Finish with cream
    text: Pour in the cream, stir and simmer for 2 minutes. Add the drained pasta with a splash of cooking water and toss until every strand is coated.
  - title: Serve
    text: Stir through the parmesan, season with black pepper and tear over the basil. Serve straight away.

The sauce thickens quickly once the cream goes in. If it looks tight, loosen it with a little more of the reserved pasta water rather than extra cream.
