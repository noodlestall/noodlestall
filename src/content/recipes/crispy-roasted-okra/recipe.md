    ingredients:
      - name: Olive oil
        quantity: 30

      - name: Kosher salt
        quantity: 4.5
        unit: g
      - name: Cayenne pepper
        note: ¼ teaspoon
        optional: true
      - name: Garlic powder
        note: ¼ teaspoon
      - name: Smoked paprika
        note: ¼ teaspoon
      - name: Okra
        quantity: 450
        unit: g
        note: fresh, small to medium pods
instructions:
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: Set a rack in the middle of the oven and heat it to 230 °C.
  - title: Mix the seasoning
    text: Whisk the olive oil, salt, cayenne, garlic powder and smoked paprika in a large bowl until the salt has dissolved.
  - title: Prepare the okra
    text: Rinse the okra and pat dry. Trim the stem ends, halve the pods lengthways and toss them in the bowl until evenly coated. Spread on a rimmed baking tray in a single layer, cut side down.
  - title: Roast
    text: Roast for about 15 minutes, until the undersides are browned and the edges are lightly crisp.
