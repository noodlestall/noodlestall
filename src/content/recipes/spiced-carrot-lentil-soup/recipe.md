description: A quick, warming soup of grated carrot and red lentils with toasted cumin and chilli, finished with yoghurt.

servings: 4

ingredientGroups:
  - title: Ingredients
    ingredients:
      - name: Cumin seeds
        note: 2 teaspoons
      - name: Chilli flakes
        note: a pinch
      - name: Olive oil
        quantity: 30
        unit: ml
      - name: Carrots
        quantity: 600
        unit: g
        note: washed and coarsely grated, no need to peel
      - name: Split red lentils
        quantity: 140
        unit: g
      - name: Hot vegetable stock
        quantity: 1
        unit: l
      - name: Milk
        quantity: 125
        unit: ml
      - name: Plain yoghurt
        note: to serve
      - name: Naan bread
        note: to serve
instructions:
  - title: Toast the spices
    text: Dry-fry the cumin seeds and chilli flakes in a large saucepan for 1 minute until they start to jump and smell fragrant. Scoop out about half and set aside.
  - title: Simmer
    text: Add the oil, carrots, lentils, stock and milk to the pan and bring to the boil. Simmer for 15 minutes until the lentils are swollen and soft.
  - title: Blend
    text: Blitz with a stick blender or in a food processor until smooth, or leave it chunky.
  - title: Serve
    text: Season to taste and top each bowl with a spoonful of yoghurt and the reserved spices. Serve with warm naan.
