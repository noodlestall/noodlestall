instructions:
  - title: Sauté the onion
    text: Get everything ready except the crème fraîche, which stays in the fridge. Soften the onion in the butter, then add the carrots and sauté briefly.
  - title: Simmer
    text: Add the potato, stock and ginger and bring to the boil. Cover and simmer over medium heat for about 30 minutes until everything is soft.
  - title: Blend and season
    text: Pour in the cream and blend until smooth. Season with salt and pepper and sharpen with a few drops of lemon juice or vinegar.
  - title: Serve
    text: Ladle into warmed bowls or plates and garnish with a spoonful of crème fraîche.
