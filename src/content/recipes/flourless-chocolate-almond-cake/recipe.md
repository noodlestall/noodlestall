instructions:
  - title: Prepare
    text: Heat the oven to 180 °C (160 °C fan). Line the base of a 20 cm springform tin with baking parchment.
  - title: Melt the chocolate
    text: Melt the chocolate and butter in a bowl over simmering water, or in the microwave in 20-second bursts. Let it cool a little.
  - title: Beat the eggs
    text: Beat the eggs and sugar in a second bowl for 4 to 5 minutes until thick and creamy.
  - title: Add the mascarpone
    text: Beat in the mascarpone and vanilla until combined.
  - title: Fold together
    text: Lightly fold in the cooled chocolate until the colour is even, then fold in the almonds. Sift over the cocoa and a pinch of salt and fold in.
  - title: Bake
    text: Scrape the batter into the tin, set it on a baking tray and bake for 35 to 40 minutes until puffed and cracked on top.
  - title: Cool and serve
    text: Cool in the tin for 30 to 40 minutes; it will sink as it cools. Dust with cocoa and serve warm or completely cold.
