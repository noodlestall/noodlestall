instructions:
  - title: Prepare the tin
    text: Preheat the oven to 180 °C. Grease a loaf tin and line it with baking paper.
  - title: Cream the butter and sugar
    text: Blend the butter, brown sugar and vanilla sugar in a food processor for about 20 seconds until nearly smooth.
  - title: Make the batter
    text: Add the eggs one at a time, then the crème fraîche. Peel and mash the bananas, keeping one back. Sift both flours, the baking powder, cinnamon and salt into a large bowl, tip in any bran left in the sieve, then fold the butter mixture into the flour.
  - title: Fill the tin
    text: Fold the mashed banana through the batter and spread it in the tin. Halve the reserved banana lengthways and press the halves cut side up into the batter.
  - title: Bake and cool
    text: Bake for about 1 hour until golden and cooked through; a skewer should come out clean after 50 minutes or so. Cool in the tin for 30 minutes, then turn out and cool fully on a rack.
