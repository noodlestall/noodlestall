instructions:
  - title: Prepare
    text: Heat the oven to 180°C top and bottom heat and line a 24 cm loaf tin with baking paper.
  - title: Make the batter
    text: Separate the eggs and whisk the whites with a pinch of salt until stiff. Beat the soft butter with both sugars until creamy, beat in the yolks one at a time, then stir in the chestnut purée.
  - title: Fold
    text: Mix the ground almonds, cocoa and baking powder and fold into the batter alternately with the egg whites. Spread into the tin and smooth the top.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake on the middle shelf for 50 to 60 minutes. If the top browns too fast, cover with foil halfway. Test with a skewer before taking it out.
  - title: Cool
    text: Leave in the tin for 20 minutes, then turn out and cool completely. Good with a spoonful of skyr, coconut yoghurt or vanilla quark.

The 140 g of sugar can all be plain, raw cane or coconut blossom sugar. Bake it the day before so it can settle overnight.
