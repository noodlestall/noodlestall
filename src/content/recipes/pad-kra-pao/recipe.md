instructions:
  - title: Fry the aromatics
    text: Heat the oil in a wok over medium-high heat. Fry the shallots and garlic for about 3 minutes, then add the chillies for 1 minute more.
  - title: Brown the pork
    text: Turn the heat to high, add the pork and stir-fry until cooked through and starting to brown and crisp.
  - title: Season
    text: Add the sugar, fish sauce, both soy sauces and the oyster sauce and stir well.
  - title: Reduce
    text: Pour in the stock and cook until the liquid reduces and coats the pork.
  - title: Finish and serve
    text: Stir in the holy basil just until wilted. Serve at once with steamed jasmine rice.
