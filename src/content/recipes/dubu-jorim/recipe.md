instructions:
  - title: Fry the tofu
    text: Pat the tofu dry and cut it into thick slices. Fry in oil on both sides until golden brown.
  - title: Mix the sauce
    text: While the tofu fries, stir together the garlic, ginger, spring onions, chilli flakes, sesame oil, soy sauce, water and syrup or sugar.
  - title: Braise
    text: Pour the sauce into the pan over the fried tofu. Simmer gently for 5 to 10 minutes until the tofu has soaked up the flavour and the sauce has reduced a little.
