instructions:
  - title: Soften the vegetables
    text: Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Cook the onion and pepper with a pinch of salt for about 10 minutes until soft and sweet.
    image: ./step-01.svg
    imageAlt: Onion and red pepper softening in a pan (placeholder illustration)
  - title: Add the spices
    text: Stir in the garlic, cumin, paprika and chilli flakes and cook for one minute until fragrant.
  - title: Simmer the sauce
    text: Pour in the tomatoes and salt. Simmer uncovered for 10 to 12 minutes until thickened.
  - title: Poach the eggs
    text: Make four wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook for 6 to 8 minutes until the whites are set and the yolks are still soft.
    image: ./step-02.svg
    imageAlt: Eggs poaching in tomato sauce (placeholder illustration)
  - title: Serve
    text: Scatter with feta and parsley and bring the pan to the table with bread for mopping.
