instructions:
  - title: Wake the yeast
    text: Stir the yeast into the warm water with a spoonful of the flour. Leave for 5 minutes until a light foam and small bubbles appear.
  - title: Mix the dough
    text: Put the flour and salt in a large bowl, pour in the yeast mixture and mix vigorously by hand for about 2 minutes until no dry flour remains. The dough will be very sticky. Cover and rest for 15 minutes.
  - title: First stretch and fold
    text: Grab one edge of the dough, lift it as high as it will go without tearing and fold it over to the other side. Turn the bowl and repeat three more times, then flip the dough over, cover and rest for 25 to 30 minutes.
  - title: Repeat three times
    text: Do three more sets of stretch-and-folds, resting 25 to 30 minutes between each. The dough should feel more elastic every time.
  - title: Check the gluten
    text: Stretch a piece of dough thin. If it turns slightly translucent before tearing, it is ready; if not, rest another 25 minutes and do one more set.
  - title: Divide
    text: Flour the work surface and divide the dough into 6 pieces of about 145 to 150 g. Cover with a towel and rest for 15 minutes.
  - title: Shape
    text: Lightly oil your hands and the surface. Press a piece into a rectangle, fold the top edge down to the centre, then fold in half and seal the seam with the heel of your hand. Roll gently from the centre outwards into a log almost the width of your baking tray.
  - title: Proof
    text: Lay the baguettes on two sheets of baking paper, three per sheet. Cover with a towel and proof for 45 to 60 minutes until about one and a half times the size.
  - title: Heat the oven
    text: With 15 minutes of proofing left, heat the oven to 230°C fan. Put a tin half filled with water on the bottom rack and a metal baking sheet on the middle rack.
  - title: Score
    text: Slash each baguette once along its length at a 45-degree angle with a razor or very sharp knife.
  - title: Bake
    text: Slide the paper with the baguettes onto the hot sheet and spray or brush them with water. Bake for 5 minutes, rotate the sheet and spray again, then bake 12 to 15 minutes more until golden, crisp and hollow-sounding when tapped.
  - title: Cool
    text: Cool completely on a wire rack before slicing.

Stretch-and-folds suit this sticky dough better than kneading and keep the crumb open. The gluten window test tells you when the flour is fully hydrated.
