instructions:
  - title: Crisp the papaya
    text: Soak the papaya in iced water for about 15 minutes so it turns crunchy. Drain well and pat as dry as you can so the dressing is not watered down.
  - title: Pound the aromatics
    text: Pound the garlic and chillies to a rough paste in a mortar. Add the palm sugar and mash until it becomes a wet paste.
  - title: Add beans, shrimp and peanuts
    text: Add the long beans and pound just until they break. Add the dried shrimp and peanuts and pound lightly so the peanuts look roughly chopped.
  - title: Make the dressing
    text: Add the fish sauce, tamarind paste and lime juice, dropping in the squeezed lime skin for extra fragrance. Stir with a large spoon until the sugar dissolves.
  - title: Mix
    text: Add the papaya and tomato and mix with light pounds, flipping with the spoon between them, until everything is combined and the tomatoes are just slightly crushed.
  - title: Serve
    text: Plate and scatter over extra peanuts. Serve with sticky rice.

Without a mortar, mince the garlic and chillies, chop the peanuts and shrimp, dissolve the sugar in the fish sauce and lime juice and mix everything in a bowl, squeezing the tomatoes by hand.
