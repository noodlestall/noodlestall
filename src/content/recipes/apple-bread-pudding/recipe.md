instructions:
  - title: Prepare the dish
    text: Preheat the oven to 175 °C and grease a baking dish of about 18 by 28 cm.
  - title: Soak the bread
    text: Combine the bread, apples and raisins in a large bowl. Warm the milk, brown sugar and butter in a small saucepan, stirring until the butter melts, then pour over the bread and mix until coated. Tip into the dish.
  - title: Add the custard
    text: Whisk the eggs, cinnamon and vanilla together and pour evenly over the bread mixture.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake for 40 to 50 minutes until the centre is set and the apples are tender.
  - title: Make the vanilla sauce
    text: Meanwhile, bring the milk, butter, white sugar and brown sugar to the boil in a saucepan. Remove from the heat, stir in the vanilla and serve over the pudding.
