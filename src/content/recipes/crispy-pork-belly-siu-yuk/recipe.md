instructions:
  - title: Blanch the belly
    text: Bring enough water to cover the pork to a rolling boil in a wok or pan. Lower in the belly skin side down and cook about 1 minute until the skin changes colour, then flip and cook the meat side for 30 to 45 seconds.
  - title: Cool and dry
    text: Move the pork to a bowl of cold water. Scrape the skin clean with a knife, lift it out and pat it thoroughly dry.
  - title: Prick the skin
    text: Poke holes right through the skin with a pair of toothpicks or a fork. The more holes, the bubblier the crackling.
  - title: Score the meat
    text: Turn the belly skin side down. Cut two long slashes into the meat, stopping before the skin, then score across them to make squares.
  - title: Make the rub
    text: Mix the five-spice, black pepper, white pepper, garlic salt and the 3 g of salt. Rub it over the meat side and into the cuts; you may not need all of it.
  - title: Glaze the meat
    text: Stir the oil and sugar into the leftover rub and spread this paste over the meat side only. Keep the skin clean.
  - title: Build a foil tray
    text: Set the belly skin side up on a double layer of foil larger than the meat and fold the edges up into walls. This stops the base burning and keeps the meat juicy.
  - title: Salt the skin
    text: Rub the 18 g of salt over the skin and leave for 5 minutes until the surface looks wet. Wipe away the salt and moisture, then spread the vinegar over the skin.
  - title: Dry overnight
    text: Refrigerate for at least 10 to 12 hours. The skin should feel dry and almost hard before roasting.
  - title: Set up to roast
    text: Heat the oven to 190°C (205°C for a small toaster oven). Put a rack in a roasting pan, pour in the water and set the pork on the rack.
  - title: Roast
    text: Put the pork in and lower the oven to 175°C (190°C in a toaster oven). Roast for 20 to 25 minutes.
  - title: Crisp the skin
    text: Raise the heat to 200°C (220°C in a toaster oven) and cook for another 30 to 35 minutes. Check through the oven window 10 to 15 minutes before the end that the skin is crisping and not burning.
  - title: Rest
    text: Take the pork out carefully, minding the steam and the hot water in the pan. Rest for an hour before cutting so the juices stay in the meat.
