instructions:
  - title: Bloom the chilli
    text: Heat a skillet over medium heat with the cooking oil and chilli oil. Add the chilli bean sauce and sliced dried chilli and stir-fry for a minute.
  - title: Brown the pork
    text: Spread the pork in a thin layer and fry for 2 minutes, then flip and fry for 1 minute more.
  - title: Season the pork
    text: Break the meat into coarse chunks and add the ginger, garlic and half the leek. Stir-fry for 1 minute, deglaze with the sake and cook until dry. Season with white pepper, turn off the heat and cover.
  - title: Make the tare and broth
    text: Bring a pot of water to the boil for the noodles. Mix the tare ingredients with the remaining leek in a bowl. Heat the dashi to just below boiling, whisk in the bouillon powder, turn off the heat and add the soy milk.
  - title: Combine
    text: Divide the tare between warmed bowls and whisk in the hot broth gradually, or whisk all the broth into all the tare and then divide. Add a dash of rice vinegar to each bowl.
  - title: Cook the noodles and greens
    text: Trim the bok choy. Boil the noodles for 1 minute less than the packet says, add the bok choy for the final minute, then drain.
  - title: Assemble
    text: Lower the noodles into the soup and top with the pork, bok choy, spring onions and crushed peanuts. Finish with chilli oil to taste.
