instructions:
  - title: Soak
    text: Put the rice in a heatproof, microwave-safe bowl and pour over just enough boiling water to cover. Leave to soak for 15 minutes.
  - title: First cook
    text: With the water level with the top of the rice, cover the bowl with a microwave-safe lid left slightly ajar so steam can escape. Microwave at full power (850 W) for 9 minutes.
  - title: Fluff and cook again
    text: Carefully lift the lid away from your face, fluff and mix the rice thoroughly, then replace the lid. Microwave at full power for another 3 minutes.
  - title: Steam off and serve
    text: Tip the rice onto a clean flat surface and spread it out to let the steam escape. As soon as it is cool enough to handle, gather it loosely with your fingers into a serving bowl or sticky rice baskets.
