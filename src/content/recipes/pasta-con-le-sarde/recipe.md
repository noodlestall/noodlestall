category: dinner

difficulty: easy
servings: 4
prepTimeMinutes: 15

tags:

ingredientGroups:
  - title: Ingredients
    ingredients:
      - name: Large ripe tomatoes
        quantity: 200
        unit: g
      - name: Sardine fillets in olive oil
        quantity: 120
        unit: g
      - name: Anchovies
        quantity: 50
        unit: g
      - name: Shallot
        quantity: 1
        unit: piece
      - name: Baby spinach
        quantity: 200
        unit: g
      - name: Dried spaghetti
        quantity: 300
        unit: g
      - name: Fennel bulb
        quantity: 1
        unit: piece
      - name: Lemon
        quantity: 1
        unit: piece
      - name: Ricotta salata
        quantity: 20
        unit: g
        optional: true
instructions:
  - title: Blend the sauce
    text: Halve the tomatoes and blitz them in a blender with the sardines and their oil until very smooth, adding a splash of water to loosen if needed.
  - title: Fry the shallot and anchovies
    text: Finely chop the shallot and anchovies and sauté them together, then stir in the blended sauce.
  - title: Prepare the spinach and fennel
    text: Roughly slice the spinach and put it in a large colander in the sink. Pick and keep any leafy fennel tops, then slice the stalks and bulb very finely, ideally on a mandolin.
  - title: Cook the pasta
    text: Cook the spaghetti in a large pan of boiling salted water according to the packet, adding the fennel for the last few minutes.
  - title: Drain over the spinach
    text: Reserve a mugful of cooking water, then drain the spaghetti and fennel over the spinach so it starts to wilt.
  - title: Toss with the sauce
    text: Return the pasta, fennel and spinach to the pan and toss with the sardine sauce. Squeeze in the juice of half the lemon, loosening with cooking water if needed, and season with salt and pepper.
  - title: Finish and serve
    text: Grate over the ricotta salata and a little lemon zest, scatter with the fennel tops and serve at once with the remaining lemon cut into wedges.
