instructions:
  - title: Cook the pasta
    text: Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil and cook the pasta until just under al dente.
  - title: Wilt the spinach
    text: Heat half the olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Cook the spinach for 30 seconds until wilted and set aside.
  - title: Soften the aromatics
    text: Add the remaining oil and the shallot to the pan and cook gently for 2 minutes without browning. Add the garlic, capers, lemon zest and chilli flakes and cook for 30 seconds more.
  - title: Add the sardines
    text: Add the sardines with a little of their oil and break them up gently, leaving some chunky pieces.
  - title: Make the sauce
    text: Pour in the white wine and lemon juice and reduce by half. Turn the heat right down, add the butter and stir until smooth and glossy.
  - title: Toss the pasta
    text: Add the drained pasta and toss continuously until coated, loosening with pasta water as needed. Fold in the spinach, parsley and black pepper, then taste before adding salt.
  - title: Serve
    text: Serve with the toasted breadcrumbs scattered over the top.
